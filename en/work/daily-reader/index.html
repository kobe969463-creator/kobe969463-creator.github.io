Daily Reader

Type
Android app + backend
Role
Design & development
Year
2026
Stack
Android · Cloudflare Workers · D1 · LLM

A feed reader I built for myself: it gathers new posts from the communities I follow every day, filtered by AI.

A scheduled backend fetches new posts daily and uses an LLM with a closed set of labels to keep only the two categories I care about; the app groups them by day.

Interaction details are tuned by daily use: double-tap to save, swipe left to dismiss, pull up to go to the next post, in-app updates.
